Help us understand the issue

When reporting a problem, please include:

  1. Your Nives version and where you installed it from, such as the Mac App Store, Microsoft Store, or a standalone installer.
  2. Your macOS or Windows version and any relevant device details, such as the affected display or audio device.
  3. What you were trying to do, what happened, and the steps that reproduce it.
  4. A screenshot or short recording, if helpful. Remove private information before attaching it.

There is no need to send passwords, payment details, or your full clipboard history. Feature suggestions and general questions are welcome too.

Common questions

Why is a hardware control or reading unavailable?

Some displays, audio devices, sensors, and battery controls are not supported by every operating system or device. Availability can also differ between store and standalone editions. Include the device model and the status shown in Nives when contacting support.

Why do capture or keyboard statistics need permission?

macOS and Windows protect access to screen content, microphone input, and certain keyboard features. Review the permission status shown by Nives and the relevant operating system privacy settings. Permissions enable local features; they do not upload your screen or keyboard statistics to us.

How do I manage saved history?

Use the Clipboard page to choose capture settings, set a retention period, or clear saved history. Use the Keyboard usage page to clear its statistics. Turning off capture does not delete records already saved. See the Privacy Policy for more about local storage and deletion.

Where are purchases and restore actions handled?

Store editions use the relevant platform store for purchases and entitlement checks. Use the restore option in Nives where available, with the account used for the original purchase. If you contact support, include the store and the message shown by the app; do not send payment card details.

Your feedback and privacy

Support is handled by email. Opening the email link does not send a message automatically. We receive only the correspondence you choose to send, along with the information provided by your email service.

For information about local app data, website hosting, and support correspondence, read the Nives Privacy Policy. Privacy requests can be sent to the same support email address.
